What remains unchanged from Annex v0.2

The six-case retrospective replay exposed over-warning in the earlier probability-trigger headline: all six cases were labelled CRITICAL. The η scores themselves separated the genuine high outcomes from the calm controls more usefully, and v1.8 introduced the traceable fixed bands above. The calm controls then moved to WARNING/WATCH while the high cases remained CRITICAL.

Honest limit. Six hindsight-coded episodes—including two calm controls—support an interim correction, not a validated calibration. The fitted model still requires the adjudicated Phase 2 corpus, a second coder and inter-rater study, equation-aligned validation, and prospective forecasts frozen before outcomes.

v1.8.1 release controls

  • All three fixtures replay automatically at startup; scoring and projection are barred if the release gate fails.
  • The workbench rejects out-of-range inputs and invalid imports rather than silently changing them.
  • Changing any score input invalidates the prior result until the analyst recomputes it.
  • Exports require period, analyst, narrative and evidence; projection exports additionally require an evidence cut-off and review confirmation.
  • Local log hashes detect continuity changes but are not equivalent to a signed server audit trail.
  • Phase 2 parameter application remains disabled until the research recurrence matches production scoring.

Use boundary

GPRM assesses pressure on systems, not people. It provides transparent probabilistic decision support, not targeting, operational orders or a prediction guarantee. Human review is required before reliance. It is not investment, legal or operational advice.
